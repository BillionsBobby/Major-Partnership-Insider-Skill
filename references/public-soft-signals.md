# Public soft signals

Add indirect public observations to the original Major research. Select a few relevant, accessible sources; keep the four layers, gamma/delta, risk checks and scenarios. No universal history window, exhaustive channel checklist or extra screening gate.

## Observation menu

| Channel | Observable changes | Ordinary alternatives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public communications | Posting/reply frequency, positive/negative wording, tentative language becoming specific commitments, recurring dates/topics | Promotion, fundraising, scheduled events, account-management changes |
| Interviews and events | Exact answer changes, voluntary dates, visible pauses/corrections, speaker mix, added/cancelled sessions | Editing, rehearsal, question difficulty, scheduling |
| Digital preparation | Public product/help/API pages, domain or trademark records, partner directories | Routine release, dormant experiment, defensive registration |
| People and roles | Hiring details, delivery/support/quality/market-access roles, public executive appointments | Replacements, reposts, stale listings, routine expansion |
| Counterparties | Customer/supplier's own docs, training, compatible products or joint-event preparation | Shared publicity, sector trends, ordinary sales |
| Costly commitments | Public equipment deposits, capacity reservations, permits, leases or procurement | Contingency planning, sunk costs, unrelated projects |
| Aggregate environment | Public/licensed area-level hotel, conference, restaurant, traffic or business activity | Holidays, unrelated events, new venues, seasonality |
| Expected but absent | An established update pattern stops; an announced agenda/product is withdrawn | Incomplete capture, maintenance, disclosure/calendar changes |

## Minimal check

- Compare the same account/topic/activity over comparable periods. Use rates with a denominator, short wording comparisons or dated document changes. If the baseline is missing or capture incomplete, do not claim a measured shift.
- Retain original URL, first-public time (or unknown), observation time, baseline/change and ordinary explanation. Filing/submission, recording and public release dates can differ; later reads do not create a historical blind test.
- Record expressed wording and observable behavior. Do not infer concealed emotion, deception or knowledge from a face, gesture, pause or microexpression.
- Ask which event the change could reflect and whether normal promotion, financing or a public calendar explains it. Check genuinely independent actors/actions; deduplicate one causal chain. Costly preparation can still precede failure.
- Cross-check the original derivatives, institutional, operational and timeline evidence. A new factual contract, permit or operating development discovered through a clue is assessed separately under the original evidence rules.
- Use public or appropriately licensed data within authorized access. No deceptive requests for confidential information, private accounts, individual movement tracing or private transactions. Regional aggregates do not identify visitors or their motives.

## Brief output

**Event/window:** one short verdict with qualitative evidence strength.
**Useful changes:** up to three sourced before/after observations; merge related clues.
**Alternative/gap:** strongest normal explanation or counterevidence, then the most useful next check.

Separate observation, event inference and any demonstrated trader-information link. Retain baseline/timestamps and follow-up notes in evidence; show the full original report format or history when requested. Do not turn soft signals into a numeric likelihood or an insider finding.
